### 🐛 Fixed

- **Phantom line breaks in received data**: text streams sent without `\r\n` were split into several lines at polling boundaries and now wrap only when the device really sends `\r\n`. Streaming UTF-8 decoding also fixes the replacement characters that appeared when a multi-byte character spanned two chunks.
- **WSL mapping with several distributions**: `usbipd attach` now takes `--distribution`, the WSL panel offers a target distribution, and mapping no longer reports success while the device lands in the wrong distribution on systems with multiple WSL installs (issue #19).
- **The WSL interface could freeze**: with no distribution or an unconfirmed UAC prompt, the elevated `usbipd -Wait` hung forever and froze the application. The result is now polled with a timeout, and frontend calls also have timeout protection.
- **Ports were not always released**: closing or deleting a monitor could keep the port busy. The release path was rebuilt to stop the read thread first, clean up with `try_lock` and wait with a timeout, and the frontend waits for the release to finish (issues #15 and #16).
- **Sending could hang** while the read thread held the port lock; the contention window was shortened and send and quick command calls gained timeouts and bounds checks (issue #18).
- **Rendering errors are isolated** so a display exception no longer triggers the disconnect and reconnect logic (issue #13).

### ✨ New

- **WSL persistent shell timeouts and process killing** were rebuilt, so a timed-out command is killed and no longer leaves a process holding the port.
- **Workflow conditions can span chunk boundaries**, and the matched action runs serially on a background thread.
- **Security hardening**: a tighter CSP, XSS escaping and SHA-256 verification of update packages.
- **WSL auto-mapping pauses after repeated failures** instead of retrying forever.
