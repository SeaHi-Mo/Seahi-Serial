### 🐛 Fixed

- **The WSL monitor toolbar now wraps** its buttons instead of clipping them when the window narrows.
- **Port locking**: one port can no longer be opened by two monitors at the same time.
- **Port occupancy refreshes immediately** across all monitors after connecting or disconnecting.

### 🔧 Improvements

- Occupied ports in the dropdown are greyed out and marked `[occupied]`.
- The toolbar group (auto-scroll, timestamps, terminal mode and so on) can wrap on its own.
