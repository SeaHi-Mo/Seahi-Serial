### ✨ New

- **Switching the port while monitoring reconnects automatically**: choosing another port while a monitor is running releases the old port and connects the new one, a failed switch stays disconnected with a notice, and switching during an automatic reconnect cancels the leftover retry, for both serial and WSL ports.

### 🐛 Fixed

- **Start-up screen**: a JavaScript initialisation failure no longer leaves a black or white window. A fallback page with a notice and reload and quit buttons, an eight-second initialisation watchdog, a safe degradation when the Tauri bridge is missing and an early global error handler were added.
- **White flash**: the window background is dark, removing the white flash at start-up.
- **WebView2 runtime**: the installer detects a missing runtime and installs it, so interfaces render on a fresh machine.
- **WSL line ending sync**: changing the line ending while connected now reaches the workflow engine.
- **Theme transitions**: switching themes no longer animates the log area row by row.
- **Dropdowns** open upwards when there is no room below, port dropdowns are no longer clipped by the window, and many monitors scroll horizontally.
- **Device mapping colours** follow the current theme instead of a fixed green.
- **Layer order**: toasts and the first-run guide cards no longer overlap.
