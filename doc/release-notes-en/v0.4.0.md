### ✨ New

**Bluetooth (BLE) debugging**

- **Device scanning and listing**: nearby BLE devices are shown with name, address, address type, signal strength and advertisement payload, sorted by signal. Scanning shows an explicit notice, supports filtering by name or MAC, and both the filter and the expanded state persist in the configuration.
- **Device type detection and icons**: the vendor company id and advertisement type determine an icon for standard BLE, Apple including iPhone, PC, iBeacon and Bluetooth Mesh. iBeacon and Mesh add a grey hint after the name, such as `NexBeacon Pro (iBeacon)`, so they are not confused with an iPhone from the same vendor.
- **Advertisement parsing**: raw bytes are shown and split by Bluetooth SIG AD type, decoding local name, advertised services, appearance, transmit power, manufacturer data including iBeacon and Eddystone, and service data.
- **GATT service and characteristic tree**: the full tree is shown after connecting, services are labelled with their standard names and anything unrecognised is labelled `Custom Service`, and characteristics support read, write and subscribe to notifications and indications.
- **Characteristic descriptors are readable and writable**: descriptors such as `0x2902` CCCD and `0x2901` user description are no longer just labels. Reads and writes follow the GATT specification, so the CCCD can toggle notifications by hand, and results are shown readably, with `0x2901` as text and the CCCD as whether notifications and indications are enabled.
- **Write panel**: with response or without, HEX or text, and no line ending, CR, LF or CRLF as in the serial monitor, with `\r`, `\n` and `\t` escapes. The window resizes by dragging, the input is multi-line, Enter sends, and the field clears afterwards.
- **Pairing support**: connecting to a device that requires pairing starts WinRT pairing, shows or asks for the pairing code, and waits for confirmation, treating sixty seconds of silence as cancel, then reconnects.
- **Data log**: notifications are shown as text plus a grey hex dump and labelled with their service and characteristic; the log clears on device change or disconnect and survives switching pages.
- **An embedded serial monitor inside the BLE page**: the toolbar button becomes a toggle that opens one serial monitor on the right to capture device logs, with a draggable width, and whether it is open, its width and its port settings persist.

### 🐛 Fixed

- **The device could not be found after reconnecting**: btleplug removes a device from its internal table on disconnect. The peripheral object is kept so a reconnect works even when the device has stopped advertising, with a short scan as fallback.
- **Writing after reconnect reported "the object has been closed"**: reconnecting replaced the underlying device while the old GATT cache survived, so writes and subscriptions used a closed object. The cache is cleared in `connect()` in the vendored fork, as documented in `VENDOR.md`.
- **The connection was reclaimed by the system after leaving the page**: WinRT's `GattSession.MaintainConnection` defaults to false, so an idle link is dropped, and it was measured to fail after 150 seconds away. The fork sets it to true.
- **A device-initiated disconnect went unnoticed**: the signal poll now also reports link state, so a device-side disconnect resets the state within about three seconds with a notice.
- **Subscription state lingered**: the subscribe icon stayed enabled after disconnecting; it now resets.
- **The connected marker leaked between devices**: the old device still showed as connected after switching to another; it is now cleared by address.
- **The detail panel was blank after returning to the page**: a connected device that stopped advertising was missing from the scan results, so its detail could not be found; it is now kept in the list.
- **Wrong device shown when inspecting a disconnected one**: the panel could show the GATT tree of another connected device. The real tree is used only for the connected device, otherwise the services advertised by the inspected device.
- **The pairing flow interfered with normal connections**: an early implementation tried pairing on every connection failure, and the ceremony occupies the device and interrupts the link. Pairing now starts only when the error is genuinely about authentication or encryption.
- **Notification logs were hex only and showed no origin**, fixed to text plus a grey hex dump labelled with service and characteristic.
- **Security: elevation command injection through the WSL detach argument**. The `busid` of `detach` was interpolated into an elevated PowerShell script without validation; a numeric-dash-numeric whitelist now applies, matching `attach`.
- **Security: stored XSS through BLE device names**. The detail panel did not escape the advertised name, so a nearby device could inject script through a crafted name; names are now escaped consistently.
- **Security: the error reporting service had no authentication**. `/report` and the query endpoints now accept `ERROR_API_KEY`, accept only local requests when unset, reject on the Worker build, and apply per-IP rate limiting and field length limits.
- **Stability**: a poisoned lock no longer panics and hangs the interface, the BLE notification, ADB output and workflow event queues have caps, and an oversized serial receive buffer reports exactly how many bytes were dropped.

### 🔧 Improvements

- **Read loops slow down when invisible**: serial and WSL monitors poll every 500 ms instead of 25 ms while their page is hidden or the window is minimised, and no data is lost, because the read thread keeps filling the buffer.
- **BLE page state persists**: the embedded monitor toggle and width, expanded services, advertisement expansion, filter text and state, and the selected device address all survive a restart, stored in `config.json`.
- **The write dialog is leaner**: redundant explanatory text and the extra close icon are gone, hints moved into placeholders, and the three rows use a label plus dropdown.
- **GATT tree readability**: names align, service type names are right-aligned, and expanding a service without content explains which of the three cases applies instead of always saying "connect a device to see characteristics".
- **Device list**: scanning is announced, and the connected hint spacing was adjusted.
- **Error messages are localised** so the cause is easier to judge.
- **Code quality**: several dead functions removed and listener leaks fixed, and CI now verifies version consistency.
