This release rebuilds the BLE write panel: it moves from a centred modal to the right side of the device detail window, becomes one card per selected characteristic, and gains a per-card loop send with an adjustable interval and a draggable height.

### ✨ New

**Write panel: one card per characteristic**

- Clicking the write icon on a characteristic row adds a card on the right side of the device detail instead of opening a modal over the whole window.
- Each card carries its own target: the header shows its short UUID and characteristic name, so writing to characteristic A and B gives two independent cards that can send at the same time. Clicking the same characteristic twice does not add a duplicate.
- Each card can be closed on its own, and the others keep working.
- A card holds the input, the write mode (with response or without), the format (text or HEX), the line ending and a send button.

**Loop sending, independent per card**

- A toggle plus an interval of 20 to 60000 ms (default 1000 ms) at the bottom of each card repeats that card's payload. Switching it off stops it.
- While looping, the payload, the interval and the three settings are locked so content cannot change mid-send; the stop switch itself stays usable.

**Draggable card height**

- The lower edge of a card is the drag handle and highlights on hover; dragging sets the card height between 150 and 700 px.
- The input absorbs the extra space, so no blank area is left behind.

**Coexists with the embedded serial monitor**

- Opening the write panel narrows the serial monitor on the right and closing it restores the previous width; the serial session and logs are unaffected. The device detail keeps a minimum width so it is never squeezed away.

### 🐞 Fixed

- All four ways to stop a loop are wired: switching it off, closing a card, closing the panel, and discovering after returning to the page that the device is gone. Every round also re-checks that the card and the target still exist and that the last send succeeded, stopping itself on failure.
- With both the write panel and the serial monitor open, the device detail could be squeezed out at minimum window size; it now has a minimum width and the monitor gives way instead.
- The card input had no visible border because it matched the card background, private 128-bit UUIDs broke the header layout, and the value `1000` was clipped to `10€` by the native spinner arrows. All three are fixed.

### ⚠️ Notes

- The loop toggle cannot be switched on by AI through MCP: it is an "write to the device forever" switch, registered so generic click tools cannot reach it. AI should use `ble_write` and pace the sends itself.
- Card height and interval are per-session state and are not written to the configuration file.
